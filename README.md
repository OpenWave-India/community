### ◈ About Us

OpenWave India is an open-source AI community bringing together developers, students, researchers, and technology enthusiasts to build meaningful AI solutions through collaboration.

We aim to create an ecosystem of independent open-source projects where people can propose ideas, solve real-world problems, contribute to shared technologies, and receive recognition for their work.

Rooted in India. Open to the world.

### ⌘ Our Mission

To advance open AI innovation through accessible technology, transparent development, and collaborative problem-solving.

### ⚙️ What We Build

- **Open-Source AI** — Practical AI tools, libraries, and applications.
- **Collaborative Projects** — Technology built through shared ideas and expertise.
- **Applied Innovation** — AI solutions designed around real-world problems.
- **Open Knowledge** — Documentation, experimentation, and shared learning.
- **Contributor Recognition** — Meaningful credit for the people behind the work.

### ◇ Our Principles

- **Openness** — Build in public and encourage collaboration.
- **Transparency** — Maintain clear documentation and development practices.
- **Accessibility** — Welcome contributors across experience levels.
- **Responsibility** — Prioritize reliable and responsible AI development.
- **Community** — Respect contributors and recognize their work.

### ⌁ Get Involved

Whether you have an idea, technical expertise, or simply the motivation to contribute, there is a place for you in OpenWave India.

1. **Propose an idea** — Identify a problem worth solving.
2. **Explore projects** — Find work aligned with your interests.
3. **Contribute** — Improve code, documentation, testing, or design.
4. **Collaborate** — Exchange ideas and build with other contributors.
5. **Maintain** — Help projects improve and grow over time.

### ▪ Current Focus

OpenWave India is establishing its community and preparing its first open-source AI projects.

Our immediate priorities are to identify meaningful problems, build useful software, establish contribution guidelines, and create a welcoming environment for collaboration.
